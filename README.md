## 🚀 Key Features & High-Converting Components

1. **Live Amazon Tag Configurator (In Header):**
   * Type your Amazon Associate Store ID (e.g. `yourname-20`) into the top navigation bar.
   * Every affiliate link on the page will automatically update in real-time.
2. **Interactive Comparison Matrix:**
   * Filterable by category (Audio, Microphones, Keyboards, Docks).
   * Direct "View on Amazon" CTA buttons with `rel="sponsored nofollow"`.
3. **Deep-Dive Lab Review Cards:**
   * **Quick Verdict & Lab Bench Score** (e.g. 9.6/10)
   * **Key Specs Grid** (Battery, Weight, Ports, Connectivity)
   * **Dual Pros & Cons Boxes** (green & red micro-indicators)
   * **"Buy If / Skip If" Audience Filter** (satisfies Google's helpful content criteria)
   * **High-Contrast Amazon CTA Button** with hover elevation.
4. **Google Structured Data (JSON-LD):**
   * Built-in `Product` and `Review` schema in `<head>` so Google displays rich snippets (star ratings, review count) in search engine results.
5. **FTC & Amazon Legal Disclosure:**
   * Prominently displayed above the fold in the top compliance bar and repeated in the footer.
6. **Mobile Sticky Bottom CTA Bar:**
   * On mobile devices, dynamically attaches to the screen bottom and switches products as the reader scrolls through each review card.

## 📝 How to Use with Google Blogger (Blogspot)

If you are publishing on **Google Blogger**:

1. Open your Blogger dashboard at [blogger.com](https://www.blogger.com).
2. Create or open a Post.
3. Switch the editor from **"Compose view"** to **"HTML view"** (`<>` icon at top left).
4. On your local site, scroll to the bottom section: **"Blogger & WordPress Affiliate Widget Generator"**.
5. Click **"Copy Comparison Table HTML"** or **"Copy Review Card HTML"**.
6. Paste it into Blogger's HTML view, add your custom text, and click **Publish**!
7. Click **"Copy Google Review Schema"** and paste it at the top of your post's HTML view so Google recognizes the product review rating.
